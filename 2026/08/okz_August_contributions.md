# okz's Caravel Contributions

Applying for a retro special contribution for my work on Caravel: https://caravellabs.net/

## What it is

A private messaging and payments app built on the Ootle (Esmeralda testnet). Encrypted chat over Nostr, confidential payments on Tari, both derived from a single seed. Message someone and send them private value in one conversation.

## How it's helped the community

* Gave the Tari community a real, working application to actually test and interact with the Ootle testnet, hands-on rather than in theory. Anyone can go to [caravellabs.net](https://caravellabs.net/), spin up a wallet, and try private transactions for themselves.
* A concrete showcase of what the Ootle can do (and acknowledged on a recent Tari Show).
* Has helped inspire other community devs to start shipping their own applications on Tari.

## Delivered to date

* Self-custodial BIP-39 wallet, persistent across sessions
* Encrypted messaging (Nostr / NIP-17), DMs and group chats
* Human-readable usernames via ONS
* Flagship feature: confidential payments fused directly into messages
* Recent additions from beta feedback: expanded relay infrastructure, message editing, and encrypted image attachments
