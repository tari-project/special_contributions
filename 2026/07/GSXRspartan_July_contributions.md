# GSXRspartan's July Contributions

A summary of contributions by GSXRspartan in July of 2026:

* Worked on selected-account stealth ScriptPath HTLC support for walletd and opened [tari-ootle #2343](https://github.com/tari-project/tari-ootle/issues/2343) to document the working testnet proof and coordinate the upstream approach with Stan
* Contributed exact stealth UTXO locking in [#2351](https://github.com/tari-project/tari-ootle/pull/2351) and `InputSelection::Specific` support for walletd stealth transfers in [#2353](https://github.com/tari-project/tari-ootle/pull/2353), both merged and shipped in Ootle v0.37
* Added walletd test coverage in [#2386](https://github.com/tari-project/tari-ootle/pull/2386) proving `PayTo::Conditions` stealth statement construction for ScriptPath/HTLC-style outputs, merged and shipped in Ootle v0.38
* Contributed to the Core Contributor governance discussion by raising concerns about sponsoring organizations controlling too much of the electorate and suggesting sponsor/affiliation disclosure, which was added to the proposal
* Raised the privacy problem with publicly attributable CC admission votes and argued for confidential voting so contributors could object without their identity being attached to the vote
* Began publicly designing an independent confidential voting approach using Tari-based cryptography, offline verification, and an eventual Ootle anchor; Fox later referenced this work when the proposal was updated to prioritize confidential voting once an adequate solution became available
